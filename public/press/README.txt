BETTERLECTIO PRESS KIT
Updated: 3 October 2026

ABOUT
BetterLectio is a free, independent interface for Lectio, created by student
and developer Jonathan Bangert. BetterLectio is available for iOS, Android,
Chrome, Firefox and Edge. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated
by MaCom A/S.

USAGE
These files may be used for editorial coverage of BetterLectio with the credit
"BetterLectio". Images may be cropped to fit an editorial layout. Do not alter,
redraw or add effects to the BetterLectio logo.

LOGOS
Use the SVG files whenever possible. PNG files are supplied at 1024 x 1024 px.
The black transparent mark is the preferred mark on light backgrounds; use the
white transparent mark on dark backgrounds. Keep clear space around the mark.

PRODUCT IMAGES
The product folders contain original-resolution browser and mobile imagery.
Images in product/web are available both as clean screenshots and presentation-
ready framed versions. Images in product/mobile are portrait campaign artwork
and a wide panorama. Images in product/features are the original clean product
captures used across betterlectio.dk.

FOUNDER PORTRAIT
The founder folder contains an original-resolution portrait of Jonathan Bangert,
founder and developer of BetterLectio. It may be cropped for editorial layouts.
Credit the portrait as "Jonathan Bangert / BetterLectio".

EDITORIAL IMAGES
The editorial folder contains six presentation-ready press images built from
the real BetterLectio interface. Wide 2400 x 1350 images cover the main product
story, dark mode, before/after and cross-device use. A 1600 x 2000 portrait and
2000 x 2000 square image are supplied for magazines, newsletters and social
media. These are the recommended images for general coverage.

CAPTIONS AND CREDITS
Suggested captions are available beside every image at
